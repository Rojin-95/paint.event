Clear responsibilities

We manage the creative experience. You provide the event setting.

Exact responsibilities are confirmed with the proposal so nothing is assumed.

Paint Events manages

  • Confirmed art or creative materials
  • Facilitation and step-by-step instruction
  • Creative-area setup and organization
  • Table protection for the activity
  • Pack-down and cleanup of the creative area

The client provides

  • The confirmed venue or event space
  • Appropriate tables and chairs
  • Access to water where required
  • Site access and venue instructions
  • Any venue-specific approvals or requirements

Corporate FAQ

Details for planners and approvers.

Can the experience be customized?

Yes. Paint Events can discuss artwork, format, timing, and facilitation choices around your audience and objective.

Can you support a company retreat or large group?

Yes. The standard request flow supports 4–100 guests. Use the inquiry form to discuss a larger activation or a format with additional planning needs.

Is the inquiry a confirmed booking?

No. Paint Events reviews availability, location, group size, format, and project scope before confirming next steps.

Do you provide proposals and invoices?

Corporate proposal and invoice needs can be included in your inquiry and confirmed directly with Paint Events.
