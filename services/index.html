Quick answers

A few details before you request.

Do guests need art experience?

No. Activities are guided step by step, and no prior painting experience is required.

Is a request a confirmed booking?

No. Paint Events reviews the preferred date, location, group, and requested experience before confirming.

Can you support a corporate or large-group format?

Yes. Visit the Corporate Team Building page for planning options, or discuss a larger public activation through the contact page.
