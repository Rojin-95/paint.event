Questions before you book?

Everything you need
to host with ease.

Still deciding? Call (949) 748-9000 or email info@paint.events.

What is included?

Canvases, easels, paints, brushes, table covers, step-by-step instruction, setup, and cleanup are included.

What does the host provide?

Please provide tables, chairs, and access to water. Paint Events confirms the exact setup needs while reviewing your request.

Can beginners participate?

Absolutely. No prior experience is needed. Instruction is step by step and guests can move at a comfortable pace.

How many guests can attend?

The current request experience supports groups of 4 to 100. Contact Paint Events to discuss larger festivals or recurring programs.

Can we choose the painting?

Yes. The guided request includes a painting selection step with options for different audiences and occasions.

How are travel fees handled?

Travel within 30 miles of Venice is included under the current pricing rules. Paint Events reviews your full location and confirms any additional travel fee before the event is booked.

Is my date confirmed when I submit?

No. Your preferred date is reviewed with your location and event details. Paint Events will confirm availability and provide the next step.

Can event details change later?

Final guest count and event details can be discussed with Paint Events before the scheduled date, subject to the confirmed event terms.
