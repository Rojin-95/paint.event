FAQ category

General

What is Paint Events?

Paint Events is a mobile creative-experience company that brings professionally guided painting and other confirmed creative formats to teams, celebrations, schools, and communities across Southern California.

Do participants need painting experience?

No. Experiences are beginner-friendly and guided step by step at a comfortable pace.

What types of events do you offer?

Paint Events offers corporate team building, private celebrations, kids and school experiences, community programs, festivals and large groups, Paint Your Pet, picnic painting, crafts and mixed media, collaborative art, and custom creative experiences. Compare the complete service options.

What locations do you serve?

Paint Events is Los Angeles based and serves Southern California. Travel within 30 miles of Venice is included under the current pricing guidance; the full event address is reviewed before confirmation.

FAQ category

Booking

How do I request or reserve an event?

Start the guided event request with your preferred date and event details. Corporate planners can use the corporate inquiry form.

Is submitting a request a confirmed booking?

No. A request is reviewed for date, location, group details, activity, and any travel or customization needs before Paint Events confirms next steps.

How far in advance should I book?

Availability varies. Submit your preferred and alternative dates through the request flow so Paint Events can review them.

What happens after I submit my information?

Paint Events reviews the request and follows up about availability, fit, required details, and the next step. No instant confirmation is promised.

FAQ category

Pricing and Group Size

How is pricing calculated?

The approved request journey provides an estimated guest subtotal based on group size and duration. Location, travel, activity, and customization are reviewed before final confirmation.

What is the minimum group size?

The current request experience supports groups starting at 4 guests.

Can Paint Events accommodate large groups?

The current request experience supports up to 100 guests. Use the contact page to discuss festivals, larger activations, or recurring programs.

Are materials included?

Yes. Confirmed art materials, step-by-step instruction, creative-area setup, table protection, and cleanup are included.

Are there travel fees?

Travel within 30 miles of Venice is included under current guidance. Paint Events reviews the full location and confirms any additional travel fee before booking.

FAQ category

Event Setup

What does Paint Events provide?

Paint Events provides the confirmed creative materials, facilitation, table protection, setup, and cleanup for the activity area.

What does the client provide?

The client provides the confirmed event space, appropriate tables and chairs, access instructions, and access to water where required. Exact responsibilities are confirmed during planning.

How early does the team arrive?

Arrival timing depends on the confirmed format, group size, venue access, and setup needs. Paint Events provides the agreed arrival plan before the event.

Is cleanup included?

Yes. Paint Events packs materials and cleans the creative activity area after the experience.

Do you need water or electricity?

Access to water may be required and is confirmed during planning. Electricity needs depend on the confirmed activity and venue setup.

FAQ category

Corporate Events

Do you provide corporate proposals and invoices?

Include proposal and invoice requirements in the corporate inquiry; Paint Events will confirm the appropriate process directly.

Can the experience be customized?

Yes. Artwork, format, timing, and facilitation can be discussed around the team, objective, and setting.

Can you facilitate recurring employee events?

Recurring dates and program goals can be included in a corporate inquiry for review.

Can you handle company retreats or large employee events?

Yes. Paint Events offers retreat, offsite, and large-group formats. Share the event scale and venue details so the right approach can be reviewed.

FAQ category

Specialty Experiences

How does Paint Your Pet work?

Paint Your Pet is a pet-inspired group painting experience. The specific preparation, image submission, and artwork process must be confirmed directly for your event.

What is Picnic Painting?

Picnic Painting is a guided creative experience arranged around an appropriate outdoor gathering and setting. View approved picnic painting photography.

What craft sessions are available?

Craft and mixed-media options are tailored and confirmed during planning. Paint Events does not publish an unsupported fixed menu.

Can Paint Events create collaborative artwork?

Yes. A collaborative format lets participants contribute to one shared work, with the concept and practical details confirmed during planning.

Can an activity be adapted to an event theme?

A custom activity or artwork direction can be discussed around the event theme, audience, and setting.

Children, Schools, and Community Programs

What ages can participate?

Can activities be adapted for schools?
