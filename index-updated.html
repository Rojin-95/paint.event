Questions before you book?

Everything you need
to host with ease.

Still deciding? Call (949) 748-9000 or email info@paint.events.

What is included?

Canvases, easels, paints, brushes, table covers, step-by-step instruction, setup, and cleanup are included.

What does the host provide?

Please provide tables, chairs, and access to water. The full booking flow confirms the exact setup needs for your event.

How much space is needed?

Enough seated table space for each guest to paint comfortably, plus a clear area for the instructor. We will help confirm the layout after booking.

Can beginners participate?

Absolutely. No prior experience is needed. Instruction is step by step and guests can move at a comfortable pace.

How many guests can attend?

The current booking experience supports groups of 4 to 100. Contact us for a custom corporate or recurring program.

Can we choose the painting?

Yes. The full four-step booking flow includes a painting selection step, with options suited to different audiences.

How are travel fees calculated?

Travel within 30 miles of Venice is included. Beyond that, the verified booking logic adds $50 for each additional 30-mile band. Your final fee is shown before payment.

What happens after booking?

You receive a booking confirmation and event details. Paint Events then prepares the artwork, materials, arrival, setup, and instruction plan.

What is the cancellation or rescheduling policy?

Please contact Paint Events before booking for the current cancellation and rescheduling terms. A published policy link will be added once approved.

Do you offer corporate invoices?

Yes. Corporate events can be quoted and invoiced based on group size, duration, location, and customization needs.

Do you work with schools and senior communities?

Yes. Paint Events offers age-appropriate guided experiences for schools, after-school programs, senior living communities, libraries, nonprofits, and HOAs.
